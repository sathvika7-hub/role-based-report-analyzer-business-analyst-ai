# role-based-report-analyzer-business-analyst-ai
Role-based Report Analyzer where AI acts as a Business Analyst. User provides report image/template with motto and small key points via prompt. AI analyzes the image and points to generate a detailed business summary, insights and recommendations with easy-to-understand breakdown.
prompt:Act as business analyst and  give me a detailed summary of this report.
1. I want report in a way that even a layman should understand,you can use bar graph,pie chart and other diagrams.
2. I also want to know the market conditions
3.And give some business ideas to start a business like this 
4. Give me some ideas which the customer wants and not fulfilled by these companies
